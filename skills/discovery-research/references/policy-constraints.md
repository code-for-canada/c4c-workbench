# Policy constraints

Identify the specific jurisdiction and locate policy constraints that the solution must meet. For governments and agencies, they often have specific published policies or even laws. For NGOs and private sector orgs, they may fall under several jurisdictional policies or laws depending on the sector and headquarters.


## What to search
Every constraint the solution is likely to need to meet in order to succeed.
- Relevant codes of practice, cybersecurity requirements, ATO requirements, privacy guidelines, accessibility guidelines.

## Output

A short summary describing the relevant policy requirements with **inline links as citations**, followed by key takeaway bullet points.

- Only include this section if you can relate it to the specific problem and what the research means for *their* decision, otherwise say there's nothing relevant.

## Gotchas
- Don't get confused by jurisdictions - e.g. just because a provincial partner falls within federal boundaries doesn't mean they follow the same legal jurisdiction. When no jurisdictional details can be found search harder or return nothing.
