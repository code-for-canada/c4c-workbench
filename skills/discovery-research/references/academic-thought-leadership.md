# Academic & Thought Leadership

What do the people who study this problem space think the right shape of solution looks like?

## Who counts

Identify thought leaders and academic authors most relevant to the problem space. Look across:

- Peer-reviewed research in relevant fields.
- Reports from civil-society organizations, think tanks, and policy institutes.
- Practitioner essays from credible operators in the field
- Open standards bodies and working groups where relevant


## What to extract

- What insights or evidence do they have about which type of solution is most promising?
- Do they point to specific solutions worth investigating?
- Do any insights suggest **user requirements or non-functional requirements** that change our understanding of the problem, or that could make or break the solution?

## Output

A short summary describing the relevant research with **inline links as citations**, followed by key takeaway bullet points.

- Only include this section if you can relate it to the specific problem and what the research means for *their* decision. One paragraph plus 3–6 bullets is usually plenty.
