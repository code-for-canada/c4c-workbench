# AI Strategy

What aspects of the problem could change with the adoption of AI?

## Framing

Find the overlap between **what frontier AI models are getting good at** and **the biggest specific pain points** in the partner's current state. Don't lean too heavily on AI magic wands. Be realistic, but also don't ignore the very real trajectory of AI capabilities. Traditional machine learning or data science is also relevant but only if a strong case can be made for the application of it.

## Two lenses

- **Use cases** — broad workflow patterns where AI can really help (e.g. document triage, customer support, multilingual support, accessibility remediation, summarization, structured-data extraction from unstructured inputs, other emerging agentic workflows).
- **Narrow applications** — a specific friction point for a specific person where a small, well-established AI capability creates outsized value (e.g. one user can now be a human in the loop while AI model does 90% of the work).

## What to produce

A short list of **1–4 candidate AI applications** for the problem space. For each:

- The pain point it addresses.
- The specific AI capabilities used (without naming a specific provider).
- The biggest risk to validate before committing.
- (Optional) An example of a specific tool or pattern, with a link, if one is obviously promising.

This is our divergent stage so include all potential compelling options, even if they have some risks or uncertainties.
